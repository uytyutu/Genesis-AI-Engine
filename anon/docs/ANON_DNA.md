# ANON DNA — unique core

## North star

ANON is not another chat, family album, or calendar.

**Core:** a person gradually leaves a **digital DNA** — voice, stories, photos, answers, favorites, memories, advice, secrets, thoughts — and ANON turns that into an **interactive “me”** others can explore when invited.

Example feeling:

> «Dad — as he was» → real voice notes, photos, answers, secrets. Not a feed. Not a bot mimicking him.

Secrets, Drops, Moments, games, Open Later — **orbit this core**. They are how DNA is collected and how it is delivered as events.

## Law

```text
DNA is voluntary · private by default · never scraped from accounts.
Hints and memories are human-authored (or clearly AI-assisted), never fake identity.
Reveal of who you are stays mutual / consent-based (Reply Economy).
Do not claim DNA Live until real capture + replay exist.
```
